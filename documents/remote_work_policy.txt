Remote Work Policy

Eligibility
Employees who have completed their probation period (typically 3 months) are eligible to apply for remote work arrangements. Approval depends on role requirements and manager discretion.

Hybrid Schedule
Employees on a hybrid arrangement are expected to be in the office at least 3 days per week, with Tuesday and Thursday designated as mandatory in-office days for team collaboration.

Fully Remote Roles
Certain roles, primarily in engineering and customer support, may be approved for fully remote work. Fully remote employees must maintain a stable internet connection and be reachable during core working hours of 10 AM to 4 PM local time.

Equipment
The company provides a laptop and a one-time home office setup allowance of AED 1,500 for employees approved for remote or hybrid work. Requests for reimbursement must be submitted within 60 days of approval.

Security Requirements
Remote employees must use company-issued VPN software when accessing internal systems and must not use public, unsecured Wi-Fi networks for work-related tasks involving sensitive data.
