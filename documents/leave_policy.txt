Leave Policy

Annual Leave
All full-time employees are entitled to 25 working days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Leave accrues monthly at a rate of approximately 2.08 days per month. Unused annual leave can be carried forward to the next calendar year, up to a maximum of 5 days. Any leave balance beyond this cap will be forfeited unless approved otherwise by HR.

Sick Leave
Employees are entitled to 15 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required for any sick leave lasting more than 2 consecutive days. Sick leave does not carry forward to the next year and resets on January 1st.

Parental Leave
New parents are entitled to 12 weeks of paid parental leave, which can be taken any time within the first year after the birth or adoption of a child. Employees must notify HR at least 30 days in advance where possible.

Public Holidays
The company observes all official public holidays as announced by the government each year. If a public holiday falls on a weekend, no substitute day off is provided unless stated otherwise by local labor law.

Requesting Leave
All leave requests must be submitted through the HR portal at least 5 working days in advance, except in emergencies. Manager approval is required before leave is confirmed.
