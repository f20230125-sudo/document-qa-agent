Expense Reimbursement Policy

Eligible Expenses
Employees may claim reimbursement for pre-approved business-related expenses, including travel, client meals, accommodation, and necessary software subscriptions used for work purposes.

Submission Process
All expense claims must be submitted through the finance portal within 30 days of the expense being incurred. Claims must include an itemized receipt; claims without receipts will not be processed except for amounts under AED 50.

Travel Expenses
Domestic travel must be booked through the approved corporate travel provider. Economy class is standard for flights under 5 hours; business class may be approved for longer flights with manager sign-off. Mileage for personal vehicle use is reimbursed at a fixed rate set annually by finance.

Meal Allowances
When traveling for business, employees may claim up to AED 150 per day for meals without itemized receipts, provided the trip is documented in the travel request system.

Approval Timeline
Finance aims to process and reimburse approved expense claims within 10 business days of submission. Claims exceeding AED 5,000 require additional approval from a department head.
